# Subcontract Management **Complete Subcontract Contract & Progress Billing Management for Odoo 17** Manage subcontract agreements, contract BOQ, executed quantities, progress invoices and subcontractor documentation directly inside Odoo. ## Designed for Construction Companies Subcontract Management helps construction companies organize subcontract agreements and monitor execution progress with structured contract lines and progress invoicing. ### Key Features - **Subcontract Contracts:** Organize and manage subcontract agreements. - **Contract BOQ:** Define contract items, quantities and agreed pricing. - **Progress Invoices:** Prepare subcontract progress invoices based on executed quantities. - **Quantity Tracking:** Manage quantities added through progress operations. - **Tax Calculation:** Calculate taxes according to configured tax settings. - **PDF Reports:** Generate printable progress invoice documents. - **Excel Import:** Import contract lines using Excel. - **Attachments:** Store documents and attachments related to contracts and progress invoices. - **Accounting Integration:** Integration with Odoo Accounting. - **Purchase Integration:** Integration with Odoo Purchase. ## Main Business Benefits - Centralize subcontract information. - Track subcontract execution. - Manage progress billing. - Organize contract documentation. - Reduce manual work in progress invoice preparation. - Keep subcontract-related operations within Odoo. ## Technical Information - **Platform:** Odoo 17.0 - **Edition:** Odoo Community - **License:** OPL-1 - **Dependencies:** Accounting, Product and Purchase - **Price:** $1,200 USD ## Support For technical support and inquiries, please contact the developer through the support details listed on the product page.
